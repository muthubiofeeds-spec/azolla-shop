About fresh Azolla

Fresh Azolla is the whole living plant: a small floating fern that we grow in grow bags on our farms in Namakkal, Tamil Nadu. It is sold by weight at ₹70 per kg and is used by farmers as a green supplement for cattle, goats and poultry, alongside their usual dry fodder, green fodder and concentrate.

Because fresh Azolla is mostly water, it is bulky compared with the dried forms. Many farmers rinse it in clean water, drain it, and mix it into the regular feed, starting with a small quantity and increasing slowly over about a week while watching how the animals respond. Poultry keepers often chop it finely before mixing it into their mash.

Handling and delivery

Fresh Azolla is perishable, so it is best to order the quantity you can use within a short time. Keep it cool and out of direct sun until feeding. Delivery timing for fresh orders is confirmed with you on WhatsApp so it reaches you in good condition.

If you would rather keep a stock of Azolla for longer, our Azolla powder and Azolla granules are dried forms that are easier to store.

This product is a feed supplement for cattle, goats and poultry and is not a complete feed. Results vary. Consult your veterinarian or animal nutritionist for feeding advice.
