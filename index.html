About Fresh Azolla

Azolla is a small floating water fern grown on shallow water. Our fresh Azolla is harvested live from cultivation tanks at our Namakkal farm and supplied as a green feed supplement — added to a balanced diet of regular fodder and feed, not used as the sole feed source.

Fresh Azolla for cattle and goats

Fresh Azolla is commonly fed to cattle and goats as a supplementary green feed, introduced gradually alongside their usual fodder. It is not intended to replace regular feed but to add to an existing feeding routine.

Storage and handling

Because it is live, fresh Azolla has a shorter storage life than our powder or granule forms. Keep it out of direct sun and use it soon after delivery. If you need something that stores for longer, see Azolla Powder or Azolla Granules.

Frequently asked questions

How long does fresh Azolla last?
It should be used soon after it reaches you and kept out of direct sun, since it is a live product rather than dried.

Can fresh Azolla be fed directly to cattle and goats?
Yes, as a green feed supplement alongside regular fodder — typically introduced gradually rather than as a full feed replacement.
